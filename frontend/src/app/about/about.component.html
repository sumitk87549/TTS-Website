Why does this exist?

I make short-form Hindi content and got tired of paying for voice-over tools that feel built for English speakers and priced in dollars. The good ones were expensive. The cheap ones sounded robotic.

So I wired up an open-source Hindi TTS model to a web interface on my laptop, gave it a design, and put it online. No VC funding. No team. Just evenings, weekends, and a question worth answering.

"

Every story worth telling deserves a voice that does it justice — regardless of budget. Hindi is one of the most spoken languages on earth, and its creators deserve purpose-built tools.

How it works today

🆓

Completely free right now

No credit card, no trial limits, no nag screens. Free while we're testing.

🤖

Powered by open-source AI

The voice model is Supertonic, an open-source Hindi TTS engine running locally.

💸

May need paid tiers eventually

If this gets real users, an optional paid tier will cover running costs. The free tier stays.

Roadmap — if it takes off

Honest intentions, not promises with dates.

  • More Indian languages

    Tamil, Telugu, Bengali — quality-first, not checkbox support.

  • More voice styles

    News anchor, storyteller, young/elder voices for specific content needs.

  • Voice cloning (premium)

    Train a model on your recordings. Requires better hardware, so it'll be premium.

  • Multi-speaker dialogue

    Conversations between voices. Technically possible, needs careful engineering.

One thing that would help

If you've used this tool, telling us whether you'd ever pay for something like this is the most useful feedback you can give. You can do that in Settings once signed in.
